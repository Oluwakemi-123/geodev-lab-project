# Project Brief: Healthcare Facility Accessibility Analysis in Akure South

## Spatial Question
Which settlements in Akure South Local Government Area are located more than 5 km away from the nearest primary healthcare facility, and how well does the existing road network support access to these underserved communities?

## Why It Matters
This kind of analysis is used by local health authorities, NGOs, and emergency planners to decide where new health facilities or outreach programs are most needed. I'm exploring it because it's a real, practical use of GIS in my own state, and it directly builds the skills I need for the rest of this program.

## Objectives
- To map settlement locations and primary healthcare facilities across Akure South Local Government Area.
- To generate 5-kilometer buffer zones around existing primary healthcare facilities to measure spatial coverage.
- To identify underserved settlements that fall outside the 5 km healthcare accessibility threshold.
- To analyze road network proximity and connectivity to the identified underserved settlements.

## The Data I Need
- Administrative Boundary & Settlements — HDX Nigeria — https://data.humdata.org — Vector
- Health Facility Locations — OpenStreetMap (OSM) — https://www.openstreetmap.org — Vector
- Road Networks — OpenStreetMap via Geofabrik Nigeria — https://download.geofabrik.de — Vector

## What I Would Build
A map showing underserved settlements shaded by distance from the nearest health facility, with the road network overlaid to show which of those settlements are poorly connected. Longer term, this could become a simple dashboard a health officer could use to prioritize where new facilities or outreach visits are needed.
