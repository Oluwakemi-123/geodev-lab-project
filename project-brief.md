# Project Brief: Healthcare Facility Accessibility Analysis in Akure South

## Spatial Question
Which settlements in Akure South Local Government Area are located more than 5 km away from the nearest primary healthcare facility, and how well does the existing road network support access to these underserved communities?

## Objectives
- To map settlement locations and primary healthcare facilities across Akure South Local Government Area.
- To generate 5-kilometer buffer zones around existing primary healthcare facilities to measure spatial coverage.
- To identify underserved settlements that fall outside the 5 km healthcare accessibility threshold.
- To analyze road network proximity and connectivity to the identified underserved settlements.
